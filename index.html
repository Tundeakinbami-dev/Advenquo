BUILDING MANUAL

How to Build in Advenquo

🥽 Meta Quest (Seated Two-Handed VR)

• Sit comfortably in your play space facing the construction pad.
• Reach with your hands and pinch your thumb and index finger to grab any block or component.
• Rotate your wrist to align the component in 3D space.
• For larger pieces (columns, beams, roof panels), use both hands for stable two-handed manipulation.
• Bring the component near a compatible snap socket to see the emerald green snap preview.
• Release your pinch to snap the component firmly into place!

💻 Desktop Keyboard & Mouse Fallback

• Click any component in the materials palette or in the 3D scene to pick it up.
• Move your mouse to position the component.
• Press Q / E to rotate yaw (wrist rotation equivalent).
• Press R to rotate pitch.
• Click to release and snap into the assembly.
• Press U to undo the last placement.

🏗️ Structural Engineering Rules

• Every upper component must trace a continuous load path down to the foundation.
• Openings (doors and windows) require a horizontal lintel beam before supporting upper wall or roof loads.
• Roof panels must rest on beam seats or wall plates.

ABOUT ADVENQUO

Immersive VR Construction

Advenquo is an immersive, seated, two-handed VR construction and architectural engineering game. Built with Meta Immersive Web SDK (IWSDK), Three.js, and WebXR.

Experience the tactile joy of modular LEGO-like interlocking mechanics paired with high-fidelity architectural materials: kiln-fired terracotta clay bricks, cured aggregate concrete blocks, reinforced rebar columns, prestressed lintel beams, precast floor decks, and weathered corrugated zinc roofing.

Master real engineering principles: load-bearing continuity, cantilever thresholds, and structural statics across houses, duplexes, bridges, and castles.

🔨 Meta Quest 3 hand tracking active. Sit comfortably and use two hands to build.
