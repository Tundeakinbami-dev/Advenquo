GUIDE

How to Play Advenquo

📱 Mobile & Tablet

• Tap any artefact on the wooden table or tap the cards in the bottom tray to inspect its story and hear its chime.
• Fully responsive layout designed for touch-first exploration.

💻 Desktop

• Click on artefacts on the table or use the bottom navigation cards to discover all 4 cultural relics.

🥽 Meta Quest (WebXR VR)

• Put on your Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S, or Pro.
• Tap "Enter VR" to enter the seated-friendly immersive world.
• Point your hand and pinch (or pull the controller trigger) to discover artefacts and reveal 3D floating fact panels.

ABOUT ADVENQUO

The World Begins at Home

Advenquo is an original hands-first, seated-friendly VR cultural exploration game created for the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026. Built with Meta Immersive Web SDK (IWSDK), Three.js, and WebXR.

In this opening journey, explore the Yoruba culture of West Africa through 4 authentic artefacts resting on an arm-reach display table: Adire resist-dyed cloth, the Gangan talking drum, the carved Igba calabash bowl, and the sacred Obi kola nut.

Discover all four items to unlock the Nile Valley: Ancient Nubian Architecture.

ℹ️ WebXR immersive mode is best experienced on Meta Quest. Exploring in 3D Web Showcase.
